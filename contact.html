Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often.

How can I place an order?

Browse our Shop page, add your favorite accessories to your bag, then continue to checkout. You can also message us on WhatsApp if you need help choosing a product.

What payment methods are available?

Payment options are shown at checkout. For any payment question, please contact our customer service team via email or WhatsApp.

How long does shipping take?

Delivery time depends on your location and the courier you choose. Our team will share an estimate once your order is confirmed.

Can I return or exchange a product?

If your item arrives damaged or not as described, please contact us as soon as possible and we will help with a return or an exchange.

How can I track my order?

After your order is shipped, we will send the tracking details to you via WhatsApp or email.
